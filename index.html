Questions our guests ask

What documents are required for Umrah?

A passport valid for at least 6 months, two recent photographs, a meningitis (ACWY) vaccination certificate, and a completed visa application. We prepare and submit everything on your behalf.

When is the best time to travel for Umrah?

Ramadan, the last ten nights, and the days after Hajj are the most spiritually intense. For comfort, the shoulder months (October–November, February–March) offer cooler weather and smaller crowds.

How is the cost structured?

Your package price covers flights, 5★ accommodation, all transfers, meals, guide fees, visa, insurance and zam-zam. Only personal shopping and optional experiences sit outside.

Can you arrange a private, family-only group?

Yes — private groups from 4 to 40 guests are our specialty. You choose hotels, airline, dates and guide language; we design the rest.

Is there support if I fall ill in Makkah or Madinah?

A resident Al-Safar team member is reachable 24/7 and accompanies guests to approved hospitals. All medical costs within package coverage are handled on your behalf.
